🎱 Campus 8-Ball Pool Rules

🎱 The Goal

The objective is to pocket all of your assigned group of balls (either Solids [1-7] or Stripes [9-15]) and then legally pocket the 8-ball (black ball) into a designated pocket to win the game.

📋 Simple Rules of Play

  • The Break: One player hits the stacked triangle of balls to start the game. If any balls go into a pocket on the break, the table is still "open" until a player cleanly pockets a specific ball to claim their group.
  • Choosing Groups: The first player to legally pocket a ball after the break chooses that group for the rest of the game. For example, if you pocket a Solid, you are "Solids" and your opponent is "Stripes."
  • Taking Turns: You keep shooting as long as you legally pocket a ball from your own group. If you miss or commit a foul, it becomes your opponent's turn.
  • Hitting Your Group First: On every shot, the cue ball (white ball) must hit one of your own balls first. If it hits an opponent's ball first, or hits nothing at all, it is a foul.

⚠️ Common Fouls & Penalties

When a player commits a foul, the opponent gets "Ball in Hand". This means they can pick up the white cue ball and place it anywhere on the table for their next shot.

A foul happens if:
  • The cue ball goes into a pocket (called scratching).
  • The cue ball does not touch any ball on the table.
  • The cue ball hits the opponent’s ball or the 8-ball first.

🏆 Winning and Losing

  • How to Win: Once you have pocketed all 7 of your group's balls (Solids or Stripes), you must pocket the black 8-ball into a designated pocket to win.
  • Instant Loss: You lose the game instantly if you pocket the 8-ball before clearing your group, or if you scratch (pocket the white cue ball) on the same turn that you pocket the 8-ball, or if you pocket the 8-ball into the wrong pocket.
